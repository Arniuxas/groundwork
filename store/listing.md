## English

**Name** (30): Groundwork: Leadership Game

**Subtitle, App Store** (30): Learn to lead a real team

**Short description, Google** (80): Run a team in a busy office, make hard calls and learn how leadership works.

**Promotional text, App Store** (170): The first two weeks are free. Talk to your people, settle conflicts, split the bonus and watch how every decision shapes your team.

**Keywords, App Store** (100): manager,leadership,team,management,training,simulator,Lencioni,coaching,conflict,boss

**Description:**

Groundwork is a game for managers. Your team works in the office: people get tired, argue, make mistakes and come to you. Each day takes about 10 minutes, and every decision has consequences, sometimes days later.

WHAT YOU DO
• Talk to people and find out what motivates each of them
• Face real manager dilemmas: an angry client, a raised target, a kitchen fight, splitting the bonus
• Run meetings, hire new people, promote a team lead
• Later, lead two teams through other leads

WHAT YOU LEARN
The game is built on Patrick Lencioni’s five dysfunctions of a team: trust, conflict, commitment, accountability and results. After each decision you get a short insight into why it worked or didn’t. At the end of each week you get a leadership profile and a task for your real job.

• A 3-minute leadership test shows where your team is strongest
• 229 insight cards
• 5 career weeks, then endless challenges that keep getting harder
• Lithuanian and English

PRICE
The first two weeks are free. The full version is a single purchase: no subscription and no ads. We collect no data; everything stays on your phone.
